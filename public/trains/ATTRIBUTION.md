https://library.ldraw.org/omr). OMR models are redistributable under the Creative Commons Attribution 2.0

## Procedural models

### 60052-1 Cargo Train

No LDraw model of 60052 exists in the LDraw Official Model Repository (all 1470 OMR sets listed via library.ldraw.org/omr/sets, Oct 2026) or as an openly licensed file elsewhere (GitHub code search, LDraw forums, Eurobricks). This is a procedural model, not a measured one. Real LDraw library parts set the running gear: chassis parts from the set's element inventory for 60052 (Brickset inventory: 2x 92339 Train Base 6x28 for the locomotive and fuel wagon, 1x 92088 Train Base 6x24 for the cable drum wagon; the cattle wagon is built on 2x14/6x plates, modelled as 14 studs), 87574 infrared train motor with its wheels (55423 stands in for element 57999), 57878 wheels on 4x6 bogie plates (18626) with 2878 holders, 50254 small train wheels on the cattle wagon, magnetic buffer beams 29084 at every end (29082 has no LDraw part). Bogie pivots sit in the centre hole of each chassis' 3-hole group (92339 +/-72 mm, 92088 +/-56 mm; +/-8 mm uncertainty), bogie axle spacing 24 mm and the cattle wagon axles are estimates read from the building instructions (lego.com 6124197, 6124199, 6124203, 6201476, 6201479, 6201485). Body heights follow the official product page (lego.com, 2018): locomotive 3 in high, 11 in long, 2 in wide; fuel wagon 3 x 9 x 2 in; cable drum wagon 3 x 8 x 2 in; cattle wagon 2 in high, 5 in long. Lengths come from the chassis parts plus buffer beams (the locomotive measures 23.6 cm over couplers against the product page's rounded 11 in). Cab, hood, tank, drums and cage are boxes and cylinders sized from the instructions: estimates.
