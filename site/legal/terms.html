Your account

  • You must be at least 16 to create an account. One account per person.

  • Your display name is public on everything you share. It must not impersonate someone else or be offensive.
  • You can delete your account at any time from your account page.

Using Plateway fairly

Plateway runs on a small shared server, so preparing plates has a daily limit and one prepare runs at a time. Prepared files are kept for 7 days. Please don't:

  • try to get around limits, queues or access controls, or reach other people's data;
  • attack, overload, probe or scrape the service, or automate it in ways a person using the app would not;
  • upload malware, or anything unlawful, hateful, sexual or meant to harass;
  • upload anything you don't have the right to share.

We may remove content or suspend an account that breaks these terms. Where we can, we will tell you why.

Your projects

Your projects remain yours. You allow us to store, copy and process them only as needed to run Plateway for you: saving, checking, preparing plates and making exports. Private projects are never shown to anyone else.

Sharing layouts

When you publish a layout to the gallery, you license it to everyone under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0), credited to your display name. Anyone may open, remix, print and share it, also commercially, as long as they credit you. A remix keeps the credit to the original. Before publishing, Plateway removes your owned-track inventory, test-drive notes, fit calibration and print setup from the shared copy.

A CC BY licence cannot be withdrawn for copies people already made. You can remove your layout from the gallery, or delete your account to remove all of them; remixes others made stay, with their credit to you.

Contributing pieces, train models and furniture

When you upload a printable piece, a train model or a furniture preset, you confirm that:

  • you made it, or otherwise have the right to share it under the licence below;
  • it does not infringe anyone's copyright, trademark or other rights; and
  • if it is adapted from someone else's openly licensed work, you have named the source and its authors.

Licence. You license each upload to everyone under CC BY 4.0, credited to your display name. A train model adapted from another CC BY model keeps that model's CC BY licence (for example CC BY 2.0 for models from LDraw) and credits its source and authors alongside you.

Review. Alex looks at every upload in Plateway and may approve or reject it, and may take an approved item down later. A rejected upload never leaves Plateway. An approved upload becomes a pull request on Plateway's public library repository on GitHub, with an automatic check; merging it adds it to the library, closing it rejects it.

Permanence. Once approved, the upload and your display name are public on GitHub permanently, whether the pull request is merged or closed, and even after you delete your account. Items merged into the library stay in it, credited to your display name as it was.

Contributed pieces print exactly as uploaded: Plateway does not regenerate them for your print style or fit adjustment.

Takedown requests

If you believe something in the gallery or the library infringes your copyright or other rights, or is unlawful, email the takedown contact: plateway@alexcarvalho.me. Please include:

  1. a link to the layout or library item, or its name;
  2. which rights it infringes and why, and who holds them;
  3. your name and how to reach you; and
  4. a statement that you believe in good faith the use is not authorised, and that your request is accurate.

We aim to respond within 7 days. If the request holds up, we hide the layout or remove the item from Plateway's library and from the library repository's current files, and tell the person who shared it, who may reply. Earlier versions can remain in the repository's history on GitHub; you can also ask GitHub directly through its takedown policy. For other problems with a shared layout, use the Report button on it.

Printed parts

Plateway checks layouts and plates digitally only. A clean check, a successful slice or a good calibration sample does not guarantee that printed parts will fit, hold, carry a train or be safe. Print the calibration sample, test-fit a joint and run a wagon over every route before you rely on a layout. You print and use parts at your own risk; printed parts can have sharp edges and small pieces, so keep them away from young children.
