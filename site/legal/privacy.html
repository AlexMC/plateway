Privacy policy

Last updated 10 October 2026

In short

  • You can design, check and test drive without an account. That work stays in your browser.
  • With an account we store your email, display name, projects and print results, only to run Plateway for you.
  • Describe track sends your text and your own Anthropic key from your browser straight to Anthropic. Plateway never sees the key.
  • Shared layouts and approved contributions are public under your display name. Once an upload is approved it stays public on GitHub permanently, even after you delete your account.
  • No ads and no tracking. We count page views with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which sets no cookies and builds no profile of you. The app also counts, per day, how often its features are used and which errors browsers hit, with nothing that identifies you. You can export or delete your data at any time from your account.

Who is responsible

Plateway is run by Alex Carvalho, an individual based in Portugal (European Union), who is the data controller for the personal data described here. Contact: plateway@alexcarvalho.me.

Using Plateway without an account

Designing, Checks, Test drive and browsing the gallery run in your browser. Your current layout, your setup choices and a few interface preferences are kept in your browser's local storage so they survive a reload; they are not sent to us. A few features ask our server to compute something, such as the shopping list for a layout. We use what you send only to answer that request and do not keep it.

Like every web server, ours sees your IP address and browser details when you load a page. Request logs are used only to keep the service running and secure.

Describe track and Anthropic

Describe track uses your own Anthropic API key. Your browser sends your description, the layout it needs and your key directly to Anthropic (api.anthropic.com, United States). Nothing passes through Plateway's server, and we never see or store your key.

Your key stays in your browser for the current tab session, or on this device if you tick "remember". That use of Anthropic is between you and Anthropic under your own Anthropic account and its terms; see Anthropic's privacy policy. Don't put personal information in your description.

Who processes data for us

These service providers process personal data on our behalf, under data-processing terms that bind them to our instructions:

  • Hetzner Online GmbH (Germany): the server that runs Plateway and holds its database, in Hetzner's Helsinki data centre (Finland, EU).
  • Cloudflare, Inc. (United States, with EU data centres): the secure connection to Plateway, storage (R2) for database backups, prepared files and the public library files, sending the account emails, and page-view counts (Web Analytics).
  • GitHub, Inc. (United States): hosts Plateway's public library repository, where approved contributions are published.

Where a provider handles data outside the European Economic Area, the transfer relies on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. We do not sell your data or share it with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

Page-view counts

To know whether people use Plateway, each page loads Cloudflare Web Analytics' script (static.cloudflareinsights.com), which reports the page address, the referring page, your browser and device type, the country derived from your IP address, and page load timings. It sets no cookies, uses no local storage and does not identify or follow you across sites; we only see totals. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in knowing how much Plateway is used. A content blocker that blocks the script has no effect on how Plateway works. Separately, Plateway counts sign-ups, prepares and contributions from its own database, as totals only.

Usage counts and error reports

The app tells Plateway's server which features a page load used, such as starting from an example, starting a test drive, opening Print or remixing a layout. Each feature counts once per page load. When something breaks in your browser, the app also sends the error message, where in Plateway's code it happened (a file and line, or a part of the interface) and the app version, at most five reports per page load. Neither carries a cookie or any identifier, and the server keeps only daily totals: how many page loads used each feature, and how often each error happened. Your IP address is not stored; it is held briefly in memory only to limit how many reports one address can send. Nothing links a count or a report to you. The totals are kept for 400 days. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in knowing which parts of Plateway are used and fixing what breaks. With Do Not Track turned on in your browser, the app sends neither.

Feedback

The Feedback button in the app and the Send feedback page on this site send us your message, the email address you give if you want a reply (optional), the page or app view you sent it from and the app version. If you are signed in, it is also linked to your account. Your IP address is not stored; it is held briefly in memory only to limit how many messages one address can send. We store the feedback in Plateway's database and email it to Plateway's admins through Cloudflare (above), so we can read it, fix what it reports and reply if you asked. We use your email address only to reply to that feedback. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in improving Plateway.

Feedback is deleted automatically 12 months after you send it, or sooner if you ask us at the contact address above. The copy emailed to the admins is deleted by hand once we have dealt with it. Deleting your account does not delete your feedback: it stays until then, no longer linked to your account.

What becomes public permanently

Shared layouts are public from the moment you publish them, under CC BY 4.0 and credited to your display name. Deleting your account removes them from the gallery. Remixes other people made keep the credit to your display name as it was.

Pieces, train models and furniture you upload are not public at first. Alex looks at each upload in Plateway. A rejected upload never leaves Plateway.

Once Alex approves an upload, it and your display name are published on GitHub as a pull request on Plateway's public library repository. From then on they are public on GitHub permanently, whether the pull request is merged or closed, and even after you delete your account: GitHub keeps every pull request and its files visible, and we cannot erase them. A merged item also stays in Plateway's library, credited to your display name as it was, as its CC BY licence allows. If an item has to come down for legal reasons, see the takedown process: we remove it from Plateway's library and from the repository's current files, but its history on GitHub may remain.

Cookies and storage on your device

We set one cookie, __Host-plateway, only when you sign in. It is strictly necessary to keep you signed in. Your browser's local and session storage hold your current draft, interface preferences and, if you use Describe track, your Anthropic key. Signing out clears the draft and the key. There are no advertising or analytics cookies. The only third-party script is Cloudflare's cookieless page-view counter (above); our fonts are served by Plateway itself. That is why there is no cookie banner.

Your rights

  • Access and portability: in the app, open your account and choose Export my data to download a ZIP of your account, projects with every revision, calibration results, receipts and contributions.
  • Correction: change your display name or email from your account.
  • Erasure: choose Delete account and confirm with your password. This deletes your account, projects, revisions, calibration results, receipts, download records, prepared files and shared gallery layouts, cancels your queued prepares and withdraws contributions that are not yet decided. What stays is described in What becomes public permanently, and security logs until they are deleted 90 days after the event. Backups of the database are kept for 30 days; deleted data leaves the backups within 30 days.
  • Objection and restriction: you can object to processing based on legitimate interest, or ask us to restrict processing, by emailing us.

For anything else, email plateway@alexcarvalho.me. We reply within one month.

You can also complain to the Portuguese data-protection authority, the CNPD (Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados, www.cnpd.pt), or to the authority where you live.

Security

Passwords are stored only as argon2id hashes, and session and email-link tokens only as hashes. All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS). Backups are encrypted, and server access is limited to Alex. If a breach puts you at risk, we will tell you and the CNPD as the law requires.

Age

You must be at least 16 to create an account.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will say so in the app and, for account holders, by email before the change applies. The date at the top shows the latest version.
